2026

AI Data Centres: Power Burden or Future Grid Flexibility Backbone?

Role: Speaker and Panelist | Event: London Climate Action Week | Location: The ZERO Institute, University of Oxford

I presented our latest research on data-centre grid intelligence and compute flexibility in a panel chaired by Prof. Thomas Morstyn. The discussion brought together Vince Colby (Pure Data Centres Group), Frederik Drewes (Loadwise), and Ethan Tiao (Emerald AI), connecting academic research with data-centre operations and flexibility demonstrations.

The Q&A covered grid-balancing cost allocation, data-centre water use, flexible grid connections, and how compute flexibility could help bring resilient digital infrastructure to developing regions.

XXIV Power Systems Computation Conference (PSCC 2026)

Role: Presenter | Date: 8–12 June 2026 | Location: Limassol, Cyprus

I presented our work on Supervised Reinforcement Learning for the Coordination of Distributed Energy Resources, connecting supervised pre-training and reinforcement-learning fine-tuning for more efficient and robust DER coordination. The conference also provided a great opportunity to exchange ideas with the international power-systems computation community.
